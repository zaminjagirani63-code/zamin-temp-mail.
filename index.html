Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is this service free?

The public GrabMail API is available without an API key, subject to its published rate limits. Vercel Hobby also has usage limits.

Why has my inbox not received a message?

Some websites block disposable email domains. Check the spelling, wait for delivery, then refresh. GrabMail cannot guarantee that every sender will accept a disposable address.

Are my messages private?

No. This is a public disposable mailbox. Anyone who knows the address may be able to read its messages. Do not use it for banking, personal accounts, password resets or sensitive information.

How long do messages stay available?

GrabMail says messages are automatically deleted five days after arrival. You can also delete a message from its message view.
